**(b) No AI Model Training.** Your uploaded child photographs will **not** be used to train, fine-tune, or improve any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, including but not limited to image generation models, facial recognition systems, or any other AI system.

By using the Service, you explicitly consent to the following storage practices:

**(a) Image Storage.** Your uploaded photograph will be stored securely on our cloud infrastructure (Microsoft Azure Blob Storage). This storage is necessary to avoid repeated image processing, reduce computational resource consumption, and improve the speed and reliability of the storybook generation pipeline.

**(b) Caching.** Processed and intermediate image data (including face-blended and cartoonised versions of the uploaded photograph) may be retained in our cache store to avoid redundant computation when the same user regenerates or re-downloads their storybook.

**(c) Retention Period.** Uploaded images and cached data will be retained for a maximum of **12 months** from the date of last account activity. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at **privacy@storyme.app**.

**(d) Deletion on Request.** Upon a valid deletion request, all originals and cached derivatives of the uploaded image will be permanently deleted from our systems within **30 days**, except where retention is required by law.

**(a) Consent to Cartoonised Likeness.** You explicitly consent to the placement of a cartoonised, illustrated, or AI-stylised version of the child's photograph on the **cover page** and relevant interior pages of the generated storybook.

**(b) Nature of Transformation.** The image used in the storybook is an artistic, cartoon-style rendering derived from the uploaded photograph. It is not a photographic reproduction. The resulting illustration is designed to be age-appropriate, safe, and used solely within the personalised storybook product.

**(c) Ownership of Generated Content.** The personalised storybook, including all generated illustrations incorporating the cartoonised likeness, is provided to you for personal, non-commercial use only.
